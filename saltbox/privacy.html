What happens to your message

Saltbox Digital is a one-person studio in East Falmouth, Massachusetts. The contact form is how you reach Matthew.

The form asks for your name, business name, email, phone, type of business, and a message. That information is used to reply and, if we work together, to prepare a quote. It is not sold.

Messages are stored by the site host so they can be read and answered. Hosting logs may include the IP address and browser that submitted the form, which is used to keep the site reliable and to filter spam.

The sample builds under Work run in your browser. Bookings and orders you place there are not saved and are not real purchases.

To ask about a message you already sent, use the contact form again and say what you want changed or deleted.
