Adulted privacy policy

Effective October 6, 2026

This policy explains what information the Adulted app and website collect, how it's used, and the choices you have. "Adulted," "we," and "us" mean the developer of Adulted, based in Massachusetts, USA.

The short version

  • There are no accounts. We don't keep a profile on you.
  • Your profile, checklist, progress, and settings stay on your device.
  • When you use Ask Anything, your question and a few profile details are sent to our server and to Anthropic (the company that makes Claude) to write the answer. We don't store them.
  • We don't sell your information, show ads, or use tracking or analytics tools.

Information that stays on your device

These are saved in your phone's app storage or your browser's storage. We can't see them:

  • Your profile: first name, state, where you live, life stage, and car year, make, and model, if you enter them
  • Your checklist progress, the guides you've finished, and recently opened guides
  • How many Ask Anything questions you've used today

To delete this information, tap "Delete my data on this device" on the Me screen, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app.

Information sent when you use Ask Anything

We send your question, plus your state, where you live, life stage, and car (if you entered them) so the answer fits your situation. We never send your name. This goes to our server, which passes it to Anthropic's Claude to write the answer. We don't save or log what you type or the answer. Anthropic processes it as our service provider. Under its commercial terms, Anthropic doesn't use it to train its models and deletes it within 30 days, except where it must keep it longer for safety or legal reasons. See Anthropic's privacy policy.

Please don't type your full name, address, phone number, or ID numbers into Ask Anything. They aren't needed.

Reports about AI answers

If you tap "Report this answer," we save the answer, the reason you picked, any note you add, and the date. We don't attach your name or device. We use reports only to fix bad answers and delete them after 12 months.

Email list

If you join the launch list, we keep your email address to tell you about Adulted, such as when the phone app is ready. We don't share it. To be removed, email us.

Information our servers handle automatically

Our server and our host, Render, receive your IP address and basic device information when you connect. We use IP addresses briefly, in memory, to limit how many requests one person can make. Our logs record only which feature was used, whether it worked, and how long it took, not what you typed.

Purchases

When paid plans open, payments are handled by Google Play (or Apple on iPhone). We don't receive your card details.

Who we share information with

Only the service providers that run Adulted: Anthropic (AI answers), Render (hosting), and Neon (database for the email list and reports). We may share information if the law requires it or to protect someone's safety. We don't sell or rent personal information.

Safety information

Adulted gives general information, not professional, medical, or legal advice. In an emergency, call 911. For thoughts of suicide or self-harm, call or text 988.

Children and teens

Adulted is meant for people 13 and older. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and note big changes in the app.
